# Business Sage — brand guide

## Identity

**Brand:** BUSINESS SAGE
**Positioning:** thoughtful AI-assisted business intelligence, financial analysis and growth strategy.
**Voice:** concise, rigorous, clear about assumptions. Avoid guaranteed profits, fictional endorsements, university affiliation claims or exaggerated automations.
**Tagline:** Strategy. Finance. Growth.
