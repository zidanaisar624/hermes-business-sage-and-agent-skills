# Business, Academic & Regulatory Accuracy Notes

1. **Respect for Business Students, Academics & Licensed Practitioners**:
   - References in `skills/business-sage/` are compiled from publicly available internet syllabi, open lectures, and regulatory summaries inspired by Financial Literacy, undergraduate (S1 BBA/FEB), and graduate (S2 MBA/MM) business curricula.
   - Terms such as "S1–S2" or "Eternal-Student / Ph.D. research loop" describe structured study and hypothesis-logging workflows—**never** accredited academic degrees, institutional affiliations, or equivalence to human business graduates and experienced entrepreneurs.

2. **Tax, Legal & Financial Scenario Disclaimers**:
   - Discussions of `PP No. 7 Tahun 2021`, `PP No. 20 Tahun 2026`, `Pasal 31E UU PPh`, `OSS-RBA`, `SAK IAI`, `BPJPH Halal`, `DSN-MUI` Sharia contracts, and `PP No. 35 Tahun 2021` are **educational starting points and scenario illustrations**, not formal tax, audit, investment, or legal advice.
   - Always verify specific taxpayer facts, spouse/entity turnover aggregation rules, cooperative eligibility windows, and latest implementing regulations (`PMK` / `Perdirjen Pajak` / `JDIH`) with official sources and licensed professionals (*Akuntan Publik*, *Konsultan Pajak Resmi USKP*, *CFP*, or *Konsultan Hukum*).

3. **Zero-Rupiah (`Modal Rp0`) Operational Reality**:
   - Negative Cash Conversion Cycle ($\text{CCC} < 0$) models minimize upfront inventory/COGS capital risk, but real businesses still carry founder time, fulfillment obligations, contractual responsibilities, and reputational risk.
